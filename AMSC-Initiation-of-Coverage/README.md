## What I found

1. **Growth came from an acquisition.** Revenue grew 30% in the June 2026 quarter, but only 3% without Comtrafo.
2. **Profit is thin.** Adjusted EBITDA margin is 6.4% over the last twelve months, while peers earn about 18%. Of the $133.8M net income in the year to March 2026, $117.1M was a non-cash tax gain.
3. **The price already expects success.** Even my Bull case ($24.45 a share) is below the $30.01 price.
4. **The balance sheet is a real strength.** AMSC has $143.7M of cash, no debt, and about $625M of US tax losses.

## How I valued it

- **DCF (50% weight):** Bear, Base and Bull cases give $5.45, $11.47 and $24.45 a share. I used a WACC of 13.15% and terminal growth of 3%.
- **Peer EV/Revenue (25% weight):** $21.84 to $41.05 a share.
- **Peer EV/EBITDA (25% weight):** $12.32 to $15.45 a share.
- **Peers:** Powell Industries, Preformed Line Products, Hubbell and Hammond Power Solutions.
- **Stress test:** The Base value stays between $9.64 and $14.85 when WACC runs from 11.15% to 15.15% and terminal growth from 2% to 4%. It stays below the price.
